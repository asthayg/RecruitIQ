## AI Recruitment Intelligence Platform

🎓 Final-Year Project Title
RecruitIQ: An AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence Platform for Resume Screening, ATS Evaluation, Candidate Ranking, and Personalized Career Guidance.

RecruitIQ is an AI-powered recruitment platform designed for both Students and HR Professionals.

It automates resume screening using Artificial Intelligence and Applicant Tracking System (ATS) principles.

• ATS Score Analysis

• Resume Skill Matching

• Missing Skill Detection

• AI Interview Questions

• AI Project Recommendations

• Personalized Learning Roadmap

• Upload Multiple Resumes

• Candidate Ranking

• ATS Based Screening

• AI Hiring Recommendation
